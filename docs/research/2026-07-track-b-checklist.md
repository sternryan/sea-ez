## 4. Insurance note

- **USUA 3rd-party liability insurance program**, administered by First Flight Insurance Group (Kitty Hawk, NC) — available to USUA members and to holders of any FAA-recognized ultralight airmen registration (USUA, EAA, ASC), no re-registration required. [usua.org/Insurance](https://www.usua.org/Insurance/)
- **EAA Insurance Solutions** (via Acrisure Aerospace) — explicitly lists **seaplanes and amphibians** among covered categories, alongside experimental/one-of-a-kind aircraft. [eaa.org aircraft insurance](https://www.eaa.org/eaa/eaa-membership/eaa-member-benefits/insurance/eaa-aircraft-insurance-in-us) — this is the more relevant lead for a custom amphibious hull later in Track A, and worth an early quote call even during Track B to understand how a scratch-built design gets priced versus a kit-built Firefly.
- **General cost shape** (not US/ultralight-specific — sourced from a European ultralight-insurance calculator and general light-aircraft insurance guides, tag as **directional only**): liability premiums scale with aircraft weight/MTOW and can start in the low hundreds of dollars per year for light aircraft; hull insurance is typically priced as 3–5% of aircraft value per year, with claims-free history able to earn meaningful discounts over time. [ul-flugzeugmarkt.de](https://ul-flugzeugmarkt.de/en/versicherung) · [SkyWatch light aircraft insurance](https://www.skywatch.ai/blog/5-data-backed-factors-that-shape-your-light-aircraft-insurance-premium)
- **Action:** get an actual US quote from EAA Insurance Solutions (Acrisure) for a used Firefly once a specific listing is in hand — hull value drives the premium and none of the above sources give a real US-dollar number for this aircraft class.

## 5. Ordering rule (restated from spec §9/§10)

**No Track A water operations (taxi, step taxi, takeoff, or landing on the custom hull) before Track B seaplane/float training is complete.** Rationale per spec §10 risk register: glassy-water landing misjudgment is called out explicitly as "the classic seaplane killer," and Track B training is the named mitigation — not a nice-to-have. This is a sequencing gate, not a suggestion: the Design-in-code and Component-proof Track A milestones (spec §9) can run in parallel with Track B, but the Test-campaign milestone's first water step must not begin until this checklist's instruction line is checked off with a real logbook entry, not just a scheduled lesson.

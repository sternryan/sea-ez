# Delta Launch-Site Survey — Sacramento–San Joaquin Delta

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Consumes:** [spec §1 (mission)](../superpowers/specs/2026-07-28-sea-motorcycle-ultralight-design.md#1-mission), [spec §9 open questions](../superpowers/specs/2026-07-28-sea-motorcycle-ultralight-design.md#11-open-questions-research-gate-inputs-not-blockers-to-planning) — "Delta launch-site shortlist (ramp grade, slough width, prevailing wind)."
**Scope:** Candidate areas per the Research Gate brief — Bethel Island, Discovery Bay, Rio Vista, Isleton, Walnut Grove. Ranked evaluation against five criteria: public ramp within ~1.5 mi of plausible housing, slough width ≥1,500 ft straight run (or open reach), prevailing wind alignment, boat traffic density, Class G/E ceiling above.

**Caveat up front:** this is a desk survey from web sources (marina listings, state boating-facility database, general geography), not a site visit or sectional-chart pull. Every claim below is either sourced or explicitly flagged `unverified`. Slough/channel widths in particular are not published anywhere as a clean number for most Delta sloughs — treat the ones below as best-available approximations, not surveyed figures, and confirm with a sectional chart + Google Earth ruler measurement before committing to a site.

---

## Candidate 1: Bethel Island / Frank's Tract

**Ramp options (public, Delta Loop area):**
- Piper Point Marina, 3861 Willow Road — public launch, 50 trailer spaces. [Visit CA Delta](https://visitcadelta.com/what-to-do/water-recreation/boat-launches-and-marinas/)
- Bethel Harbor, 3405 Harbor Road — 40-acre full-service facility. [Bethel Harbor](https://www.bethelharbor.com/)
- California DBW facility listings for Bethel Island. [CA State Parks DBW](https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/395)

**Water body:** Bethel Island sits adjacent to **Frank's Tract State Recreation Area**, a large flooded former island now managed as open water — cited at 3,000–3,523 acres of open water (sources vary slightly, all describe the same flooded basin between False River and Bethel Island). [Wikipedia: Franks Tract SRA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franks_Tract_State_Recreation_Area) · [CA DFW restoration doc, 650–1,000 acres tidal marsh context](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=159294)

**Assessment against criteria:**
- **Slough width / open reach:** Frank's Tract is genuinely open water at the scale of hundreds to thousands of acres — far exceeds the 1,500 ft straight-run bar in essentially any direction with no fixed-channel constraint. This is the strongest fit of the five candidates on this criterion alone.
- **Ramp-to-housing:** Bethel Island is a residential community built around the Delta Loop channels; multiple ramps are inside the town, so a sub-1.5-mi ramp-to-house pairing is very plausible depending on which lot is available. Unverified without a specific address.
- **Boat traffic:** Bethel Island / Frank's Tract is one of the most heavily-trafficked recreational boating areas in the Delta (marinas, water-skiing, bass fishing tournaments cluster here) — this cuts against it. High traffic density is a real hazard for a slow, low-freeboard ultralight on the water.
- **Wind:** No sourced wind-rose data for Frank's Tract specifically; Delta-wide the summer pattern is a reliable afternoon westerly funneled through the Carquinez Strait — unverified at this specific site, flag for on-site confirmation.
- **Airspace:** No known Class B/C/D overlying Bethel Island; Travis AFB Class D is well to the north (see Rio Vista section below for sourcing) and Byron airport traffic is Class G/E to the southwest. Reasonably high confidence this is Class G/E, but not sectional-verified here.

**Net:** Best water body by far, worst boat-traffic exposure. A weekday/early-morning-only operating rule would materially change the calculus.

## Candidate 2: Rio Vista (Sacramento River)

**Ramp options:**
- Delta Meadows Boat Ramp, 17645 State Highway 160 — CA State Parks public facility. [CA State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/1345)
- Sherman Island Sacramento River Access, Sherman Island Road, Rio Vista. [CA DBW](https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/1439)
- Sandy Beach Park BLF, Rio Vista city-side. [CA DBW facility list for Rio Vista](https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/City/Rio%20Vista)
- Additional private options: Delta Marina Yacht Harbor, Eddo's Harbor & RV Park.

**Water body:** Main-stem Sacramento River. The river itself carries commercial traffic — the Sacramento Deep Water Ship Channel is dredged to ~30 ft depth and up to ~200 ft wide near Rio Vista's stretch of the river, per the Army Corps channel description. [Wikipedia: Sacramento Deep Water Ship Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento_Deep_Water_Ship_Channel) — note this is the *ship channel* width, not the full river width at Rio Vista, which is wider than the dredged channel; a specific bank-to-bank measurement at Rio Vista was not found in this search pass and should be pulled from a nautical chart or Google Earth before ranking this site above Bethel Island on width alone.

**Assessment against criteria:**
- **Slough width:** Main Sacramento River channel is wide (likely well over 1,500 ft bank-to-bank based on general Delta river geography, but **unverified** — no sourced number found for the specific reach at Rio Vista).
- **Ramp-to-housing:** Rio Vista is a small city (pop. ~10k) with in-town ramps; plausible sub-1.5-mi pairings exist. Unverified for a specific parcel.
- **Boat traffic:** Moderate — Rio Vista sees recreational traffic plus the deep-water ship channel carries some commercial/barge traffic on the main river. Less concentrated than Bethel Island's Delta Loop.
- **Wind:** Not sourced specifically; river runs roughly NW–SE at Rio Vista, which may or may not align with prevailing delta breeze — unverified.
- **Airspace — the one genuinely load-bearing airspace question in this survey:** Travis AFB (KSUU) is Class D, radius on the order of ~5 nm from the field per generic Class D definition (surface to ~2,500 ft AGL) — the *exact* published radius/shelf for KSUU was not confirmed from a primary FAA source in this pass; treat as **unverified pending a direct sectional/AIM pull**. [AirNav KSUU](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KSUU) · [SkyVector KSUU](https://skyvector.com/airport/SUU/Travis-AFB-Airport). Straight-line distance Rio Vista to Travis AFB is reported as ~12 nm, which is outside a 5 nm Class D shelf with margin. [Distance source](https://www.distance-between.com/from/us/california/travis-air-force-base/to/us/california/rio-vista) Note also that Rio Vista Municipal Airport (O88) already hosts active GA traffic (home of the Travis AFB Aero Club) in this same airspace without a Class D conflict, which is itself evidence the area operates as Class G/E — pilots there are advised to contact Travis Approach for flight following as a courtesy, not a requirement. [Yelp: Travis AFB Aero Club, Rio Vista](https://www.yelp.com/biz/travis-afb-aero-club-rio-vista)

**Net:** Airspace looks clear on current evidence but is the one item in this whole survey that most needs a sectional-chart double-check before commitment — a Part 103 aircraft has no radio and no transponder requirement, so misjudging a Class D shelf here is not a "call and ask forgiveness" mistake.

## Candidate 3: Isleton (Sacramento River, south of Rio Vista)

**Ramp options:**
- Isleton Boat Ramp / Isleton Public Dock and Boat Ramp, River Road / 101 2nd Street. [CA DBW](https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/923)
- B&W Resort Marina, Hogback Island, Georgiana Slough — multiple facilities in the immediate area. [CA DBW Isleton listing](https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/City/Isleton)

**Assessment against criteria:**
- **Slough width:** Isleton sits on the Sacramento River main stem, similar profile to Rio Vista; also adjacent to Georgiana Slough (narrower). No sourced width figure — **unverified**.
- **Ramp-to-housing:** Isleton is a very small town (~800 pop.); in-town ramp-to-house pairing plausible but the housing stock/rental market here is thin — a practical constraint the criteria list doesn't capture but matters for "plausible housing."
- **Boat traffic:** Lower than Bethel Island; comparable to or slightly less than Rio Vista based on town size.
- **Wind:** Unverified.
- **Airspace:** Further from Travis AFB than Rio Vista (south along the river), so if Rio Vista clears the Class D shelf, Isleton clears it with more margin. No known conflicting airspace.

**Net:** Similar profile to Rio Vista with weaker housing availability and marginally better airspace margin. Didn't independently source enough to separate it from Rio Vista on the load-bearing criteria — the two should probably be treated as one "Sacramento River corridor" option pending a site visit, with Rio Vista preferred for housing stock.

## Discovery Bay — evaluated, not ranked top 3

**Ramp:** Discovery Bay Yacht Harbor Boat Ramp — public, 5 AM–7 PM, ~50 trailer spaces, $10 launch fee (free if car-top/carry). [onWater](https://www.onwaterapp.com/us/california/poi/discovery-bay-yacht-harbor-boat-ramp-p1)

**Why it's not top 3:** Discovery Bay is a purpose-built residential lagoon community — channel widths there are engineered for cul-de-sac boat docks, not open water, and no source found in this pass supports a ≥1,500 ft open reach inside the community's internal waterways (the actual Delta open water is reachable but requires transiting those channels first). Byron Airport (C83) is nearby but was confirmed **uncontrolled Class G airspace up to 700 ft**, no Class D — so airspace is *not* the blocker here. [Search synthesis re: Byron C83 airspace] The blocker is water geometry: this needs a direct measurement of the main Discovery Bay channel or a transit to the open Delta (adding distance/complexity) before it could beat Bethel Island or the Sacramento River corridor. Flag for re-evaluation if a specific waterfront property near the community's edge (adjacent to open Delta water rather than interior lagoon) becomes a housing candidate.

## Walnut Grove — evaluated, not ranked top 3

**Ramp:** Hogback Island Boat Launch, 14811 Grand Island Rd — noted in reviews as constrained by low water/low tide in a "small launch slough." [Search synthesis] Also Walnut Grove Marina and New Hope Landing. This "small launch slough" language directly cuts against the ≥1,500 ft criterion at the primary public ramp, and no source found supports a wider alternate at Walnut Grove. Ranked below the other four candidates on the one hard geometry criterion.

## Open items for a real site visit

1. Pull the current San Francisco sectional chart and confirm the Travis AFB Class D lateral/vertical limits against the Rio Vista and Isleton ramp coordinates directly — this survey's Travis AFB airspace claims are search-synthesis, not a primary FAA source read.
2. Measure actual open-water/slough widths at Rio Vista and Isleton reaches with Google Earth's ruler tool or a NOAA chart — no source in this pass gave a clean bank-to-bank number for either.
3. Get a wind rose (NOAA/Delta weather station data) for whichever site becomes the frontrunner; this survey found none of the site-specific wind data the criteria call for.
4. If Bethel Island stays #1 despite traffic density, define an operating window (e.g., weekday mornings) as a mitigation and note it in the Track A plan.
